A candidate is `news_driven` only if a ticker-specific story is the narrative multiple
headlines actually converge on to explain the move — not merely a ticker-specific
headline that exists somewhere in that day's pile.

Sector/peer spillover (a *different* company's earnings lifting the whole sector) is
`noise` even though it's real news, because the news is about a different company. A
ticker-specific item that contradicts the move's direction (e.g. an analyst upgrade on
a down day) doesn't get credited as the driver either.

Genuinely mixed evidence — a real ticker-specific item *and* a dominant macro/sector
driver the same day — is `ambiguous`, not resolved by which side has more headlines; a
minority but substantive contradiction is enough to make a call `ambiguous` rather than
forcing a majority-vote resolution.

The return is close-to-close, so news released after the 4pm close explains the *next*
session, not this one.
